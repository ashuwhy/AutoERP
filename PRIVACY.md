# Privacy

AutoERP collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

- It runs only after you press **Start filling** in its popup, and only on `erp.iitkgp.ac.in`.
- It reads the feedback form in that tab to fill it, and your list of subjects and teachers to show progress in its panel. That stays in the tab and is gone when you close the panel or the tab.
- The popup remembers your last tone and form choice in the extension's local storage on your computer. Nothing else is stored.
- The only network requests it causes go to ERP itself: opening each form the same way a click would, and fetching your admit card if you press that button.
- No analytics, no tracking, no remote code, no third-party services.

Questions or problems: [open an issue](https://github.com/ashuwhy/AutoERP/issues) or reach me at [ashuwhy.com](https://ashuwhy.com).
